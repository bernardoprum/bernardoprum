### Hi, I'm Bernardo 👋

Founder of **[Meryto](https://meryto.com)** — an AI recruiting platform for Mexico.

Meryto reads every job posting in the Mexican market, understands each candidate's real profile, and matches them with a single score. Companies get a ranked shortlist in hours instead of weeks; candidates get jobs that actually fit.

**What I've built (mostly solo, with AI coding agents):**

- **Job harvester** — collects and deduplicates postings from LinkedIn, OCC, Computrabajo, Indeed and company ATSs, and checks every day which ones are still live.
- **MerytoScore** — one matching engine that scores candidate ↔ job fit across role, seniority, skills, education, location and salary, calibrated against an LLM judge.
- **Itto** — a recruiting assistant that writes the job profile with the client, searches candidates and runs a first interview.
- **The platform** — job editor, candidate profiles, shortlists, video interviews, payments, and university alumni portals.
